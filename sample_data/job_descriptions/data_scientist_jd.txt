Job Title: Senior Data Scientist
Company: InsightMetrics Analytics
Location: New York, NY / Hybrid

About the Role:
We are looking for an analytical and detail-oriented Data Scientist to extract actionable insights from multi-terabyte datasets, build predictive models, and design experiments that guide strategic product decisions.

Key Responsibilities:
- Conduct rigorous statistical analysis, hypothesis testing, and A/B test evaluation.
- Build predictive machine learning models for customer churn, lifetime value, and recommendation systems using Python, Pandas, and Scikit-Learn.
- Create engaging interactive executive dashboards using Tableau and Power BI.
- Write complex, optimized SQL queries to aggregate and transform raw data in BigQuery/Snowflake.
- Work closely with product and marketing leaders to translate business problems into mathematical formulations.

Required Qualifications & Skills:
- Advanced expertise in Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn).
- High proficiency in SQL and relational databases.
- Strong grounding in probability, statistics, regression analysis, and machine learning algorithms.
- Experience with Data Visualization tools such as Tableau or Power BI.
- Experience with feature engineering and predictive modeling.
- Master's degree or Bachelor's with 2+ years of experience in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or Economics.
